What each run does Every business morning: audits all pages for titles, meta descriptions, canonicals, social cards, structured data validity, broken links, and sitemap completeness · verifies the AI files (robots.txt crawler allows, llms.txt, llms-full.txt) · rebuilds llms-full.txt from live page text so AI crawlers ingest current content including the latest rate benchmarks · commits changes, which deploys the site and pings Google and Bing via IndexNow. Separately, every business evening the rate bot refreshes the box spread benchmarks from the U.S. Treasury curve. A FAIL emails Akiva automatically.
