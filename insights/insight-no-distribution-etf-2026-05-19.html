This Might Be the Most Innovative Structure in Tax-Aware Investing Right Now.

The innovation in the tax-aware space is crazy. The "no-distribution ETF" is the perfect example.

The innovation happening in tax-aware investing right now is remarkable. The "no-distribution ETF" is a perfect example.

Here's the idea.

A standard bond ETF pays out interest income as distributions. You receive it. You pay ordinary income tax on it. Every year. Automatically. Whether you want to or not.

A no-distribution ETF works differently. Rather than receiving income and paying it out, the fund is structured so the income stays inside the vehicle. No distribution hits your account. Your investment compounds. The tax event is deferred until you sell.

That one structural change does two things. It defers the tax event until you choose to sell. And may convert the character of that income from ordinary income taxed at your marginal rate into a long-term capital gain taxed at a more favorable rate.

You control the timing. You control the rate.

For investors in taxable accounts, forced distributions are a drag. You pay the tax whether the timing works for you or not. The compounding you lose along the way doesn't come back.

The no-distribution structure stops that.

Same exposure. Same income. You decide when to pay the tax — and at what rate.

The industry is evolving fast. Most advisors aren't keeping up. If your advisor isn't talking to you about structures like this, it might be worth asking why.
